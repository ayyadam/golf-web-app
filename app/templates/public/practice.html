Practice Area

Sharpen your short game on our dedicated chipping and putting greens.

Chipping Green

Our purpose-built chipping green features multiple pin positions, varied lies, and bunker practice areas. Perfect for honing your approach shots from 5 to 50 yards.

  • Multiple target greens at varying distances
  • Practice bunker with quality sand
  • Various lies — rough, fairway, tight lies

Putting Green

Our championship-standard putting green mirrors the speed and break of our course greens. Practice your reads and build confidence before your round.

  • Championship speed greens
  • 12 cup positions with varying breaks
  • Distance markers for pace practice

Practice facilities are open to all members and available to visitors free of charge when playing a round.
